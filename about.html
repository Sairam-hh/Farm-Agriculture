Our story

It Started With Four Acres and a Question.

In 2001 our founders asked why yields were rising while soil was thinning. The answer shaped everything that followed: farm the soil, not just the crop. What began as one experimental plot in Sinnar is now a collective cultivating more than 1,500 acres.

We buy, grade, store and supply produce ourselves, so the value of careful farming reaches the household that did the work.

🌱

Our Mission

Make sustainable farming the most profitable way to farm, for the smallest holding as much as the largest.

🔮

Our Vision

A regional food system where every lot is traceable, every field is measured and every farmer is a decision-maker.

🌿

Our Philosophy

Patience with the land, precision with the data, and honesty in every transaction.

Sustainability approach

Every Practice Has to Prove Itself.

We trial, measure and only then scale. A practice stays if it improves soil carbon, water efficiency or farmer income — ideally all three.

Agriculture timeline

Season by Season.

  1. 2001

    First rotation trial

    Four acres, pulses after cereals, compost instead of urea.

  2. 2007

    Collective formed

    Twenty-two families pool grading and transport.

  3. 2012

    Drip at scale

    Irrigation redesigned across 300 acres.

  4. 2017

    Soil lab opens

    In-house testing turns advice into evidence.

  5. 2021

    Sensors and drones

    Field telemetry becomes part of daily planning.

  6. 2026

    1,500+ acres

    Full traceability from plot to dispatch.
